# DeckForge 0.2.6.1 — hand-card sizing and batched Durak attacks

## Baseline / visual reproduction

- Start from published `v0.2.6` (`f932ce3`). Keep source and release version unchanged until every gate passes.
- On the 390×844 Durak screen, the hand row uses a one-row, negative-margin fan. Neighbor cards cover each other’s lower corners even though six smaller cards can fit without overlap.
- At compact landscape widths, hand cards are too tightly packed in a narrow centered group and their corner/pip artwork is crowded relative to card size.
- The player can only select one card for `Attack` / `Throw in`; the engine has no atomic batch action.

## Behavior contract

- Scale Durak hand cards down as whole cards while retaining a correct playing-card aspect ratio and readable rank/suit. Do not overlap cards or clip either corner. Center a normal six-card hand with visible gaps; use an internally scrollable left-aligned row only when the hand exceeds available width.
- Add multi-select for human Durak attacks. A batch must contain distinct cards from the human hand, all of one rank, and fit the remaining attack capacity (maximum six and the defender’s hand size).
- On an empty table, allow a same-rank opening volley. On a covered table, allow a same-rank throw-in batch only when every card matches the selected covered target pile and all throw-in policy/turn/capacity rules remain legal.
- Execute a batch atomically: remove each physical card once, create one table pair per card, keep throw-in pile links, update turn/attacker state once, and preserve the 36-card audit. Reject duplicate, stale, mixed-rank, wrong-target, out-of-turn, and over-capacity batches without mutation.
- Durak match winner receives the ante from every configured seat: a human win returns `players × stake`; a human loss loses only the human stake; a draw/stalemate returns the human stake.
- UI selection must visibly show the selected-card count/rank; tapping toggles a same-rank card. Mixed-rank additions are rejected with a clear message. A single selected card continues to use the existing action labels and behavior. Queue-while-AI remains one-card, turn-safe behavior.
- Keep tap actions and drag-and-drop equivalent for legal moves, including dragging a legal same-rank card directly onto `Push onward`.
- The visible site version remains `v0.2.6` until all acceptance gates pass; then publish `v0.2.6.1`.
