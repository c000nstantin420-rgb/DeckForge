# DeckForge 0.2.6.2 — short-iPhone Durak and Blackjack layout

## Baseline

- Published release: `v0.2.6.1` (`2f0c2ff`).
- User screenshot: iPhone-class `375×667` screen, 3-seat Durak. The lower hand row is clipped by the table/action boundary; only the card tops are visible.
- Browser emulation reproduced it at `375×667` DPR2: Durak hand box `y=438..539` while the dock begins at `y=496` (43 CSS px of overlap); document height still equals 667 because the inner table clips.
- 3-seat Blackjack at the same size also overflows: dealer cards collide with AI rows and the human hand is partially hidden above the insurance/actions area.

## Behavior / visual contract

- Make active Durak playable on short phones: all human cards and card corners visible, no hand/dock overlap, and defense/push/take remain accessible. Use compact two-column opponent lanes for 3 seats, a one-line status strip, a slim trump/talon row, a flexible/scrollable table area, a compact hand, and one compact action row. Keep labels legible; do not shrink card faces until indices become unreadable.
- Compact Blackjack at short phone heights: dealer, each AI seat, the human hand, insurance/action controls, and settlement/rematch controls remain visible and non-overlapping. Reduce chrome and whitespace first; retain readable card corners and usable buttons. If internal scrolling is necessary, keep the human hand and current action reachable without scrolling the entire page.
- Preserve 390×844 phone and 1194×834 iPad landscape behavior; do not regress tablet/desktop layouts.
- Do not alter game rules, card identity, wager logic, or action semantics.

## Completed implementation and verification

- **Durak short-phone layout:** At `375×667`, six player cards are fully visible, hand ends at `y=611`, and the one-row action dock starts at `y=614`. Four-seat `375×600` also fits. Opponents use compact two-column status boxes; talon art and count are separated.
- **Blackjack:** Three-seat insurance and player screens pass at `375×667`; the four-seat player screen passes at `375×600`. At `390×844`, three- and four-seat AI rows fit without flex-shrink clipping. The action dock is hidden during AI/insurance and settled rematch controls use two columns.
- **Terminal state:** Finished Durak now renders `Match complete` and omits the empty talon card placeholder rather than showing an `Open attack` prompt.
- **Vision:** Final phone (`375×667`, `390×844`) and iPad-landscape (`1194×834`) screenshots were reviewed for Durak and Blackjack, including insurance, settlement, four-seat stress layouts and the Push drag highlight. Drag ghost no longer covers the target label.
- **Diagnostics/stress:** `75/75` in-app assertions and `168/168` seeded Durak matches across 14 valid configurations passed; physical-card audits passed at every action.
- **Rendered UI:** A fresh three-seat Blackjack deal/action/settlement and a full three-seat Durak UI match reached terminal states; tap and touch-pointer drag both executed a legal Push onward. Browser errors: `0`.
- **Static:** `node --check`, static audit (`263200` bytes, `2001` lines, one inline script, no external references, duplicate IDs, missing DOM references or inline handlers), JSON validation and `git diff --check` passed.
- **Limits:** Chromium emulation and synthetic touch-pointer events only; physical Safari and real audio were not tested.
- **Release:** `VERSION`/`APP_VERSION` `0.2.6.2`, notes `RELEASE_NOTES_0.2.6.2.md`, archive `releases/DeckForge-0.2.6.2.zip`, tag `v0.2.6.2`. Final acceptance: verify the GitHub release asset, Pages build and hosted runtime after publishing.
